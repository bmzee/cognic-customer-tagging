## Limitations

- Synthetic attributes and synthetic behaviour: the kit shows which pathways the rules can and cannot reach. It cannot show how a real population differs, and it never infers traits.
- Where a trait itself changes a behaviour a rule may use (for example how often money arrives from abroad), a tag on that behaviour will differ between groups. Whether such a difference is acceptable is the bank's decision under its regulator and data-protection law, case by case.
- Tags have no true outcome, so equal opportunity and equalised odds do not apply; the kit measures who receives a tag, not whether it was right.
- Many tag and attribute pairs are tested; WARN verdicts at about the shuffled rate are expected noise.
