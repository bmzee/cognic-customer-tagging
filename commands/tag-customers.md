Load the customer-tagging skill and follow its rules.

If the person named a customer, explain that customer's tags in plain words: each tag's name, its reason, the rule and the evidence, and the as-of date. If they asked why a tag does or does not apply, explain that one tag. If they named no customer, summarise how many customers have each tag and offer to explain any one customer.

If you do not know where the customers and transactions files are, ask. Use the engine's print-only mode. Never change any data, never write results back, and never infer protected traits or age from tags.
